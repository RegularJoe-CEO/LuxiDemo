Luxi Edge | NVIDIA

High-level guidance for integrating Luxi Edge alongside NVIDIA GPU platforms. Content is intentionally redacted and focused on outcomes.

Executive Summary

  • Luxi Edge is a Rust/WASM CPU offload microservice designed to complement GPU stacks.
  • It moves high-frequency small kernels to CPU to reduce GPU round-trips and power waste.
  • Designed to integrate with existing orchestration (Kubernetes, Triton, etc.).

Measured Metrics

MetricTypical Impact
End-to-end throughput (mixed GPU+CPU)+20–50%
System energy reduction40–70% (GPU-related)
Per-inference latencyMaintained or reduced vs GPU-only
Sustained ops/sec (offload portion)~15k ops/sec/node

BlueField DPU — Deployment Patterns

  • In-NIC preprocessing to reduce host GPU memory traffic
  • RDMA/zero-copy pipelines to minimize data movement
  • Telemetry and near-ingress scoring to cut end-to-end latency
  • Secure enclave placement when supported by the platform

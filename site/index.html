Luxi inference replay demo 1.0.1

Closed-binary build of the Luxi inference engine for Linux x86_64 (NVIDIA GPU or CPU). Replays a frozen DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B job, checks it against the signed November 9 H100 NVL record, verifies signed records offline and compares records across machines. Evaluation license; the engine source is private.

# Claim alignment changelog

This changelog records the marketing and status claims changed in this pull request. luxiedge.com is the source of truth.

| Area | Old wording or treatment | New wording or treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Core claim | "same bits on every chip" | "Deterministic numerics with receipts" and "Deterministic computation you can verify," followed by measured workload, hardware, date, and limits |
| Cross-platform scope | "every machine" or "every chip" in several proof summaries | "each listed" or explicitly named tested platforms; qemu and near-earth limits are stated |
| Company structure | LuxiEdge appeared as a six-layer system component | LuxiEdge is the parent company; deterministic execution is a roadmap area, not a company layer |
| Commercial structure | LuxiBook or inference copy could read as the main commercial product | The LuxiQuant Risk State Engine, Risk Runtime v0.4.0, is stated first as LuxiEdge's only sellable product; LuxiQuant is the primary commercial division and LuxiEnergy is secondary |
| Paid-product access | "evaluation-only" with a division-page link | paid confidential evaluation under NDA, proprietary, no public download, no public price; call to action points to /pilot |
| Free demos | "Luxi Book" and inconsistent versioning | LuxiBook v0.2.1 option-book pricer with Ed25519 receipts; LuxiRisk v0.2 legacy calculator |
| Energy names | LuxiPack | LuxiPak |
| LuxiLoad | Public layer or prospective engagement | internal roadmap label, absent from the current public site, not a public product |
| LuxiSDG | "Early concept" or hardware-in-the-loop next step | historical software-defined-generator work |
| Six-layer model | Presented as "The layers" with maturity labels | broader six-area roadmap, not a catalog of shipping products |
| Markets | Sector pages read as product or deployment claims | market hypotheses only, not customers, deployments, certifications, or shipping products |
| TestFort December 2025 | Historical numerical report described as unavailable | original seven-function numerical-engine evaluation linked directly and scoped to that build and workload |
| TestFort July 2026 | Prominent prior third-party inference baseline | archived historical packed-prefill lineage; different build and workload; not current validation |
| Future TestFort work | No current full-system protocol | explicit independent protocol covering Risk Runtime, LuxiBook, LuxiRisk, GPU primitives, orbital work, inference, endurance, raw evidence, and limits |
| Inference lead | Long-prompt prefill advantage appeared before the generation tradeoff | normal-vLLM generation leads: same speed within 2%, 2 to 7% more GPU-board energy per token; prefill follows as secondary evidence |
| Inference correctness | Top-token and top-five checks could be read too broadly | explicitly not full-sequence equivalence |
| Additional evidence | Kessler, SAAL, OrbVeil, genomics, and attention-v2 mixed into headline proof | retained under "Additional evidence" with workload, hardware, date status, and limits |
| Energy page | Broad efficiency and savings language | only measured GPU-board results and historical TestFort scope; no facility or lifecycle-savings claim |
| Calls to action | General homepage or division links | /pilot for "Request a confidential evaluation" and /proof for evidence |
| Patent language | Audit requested removal | no patent claim was present in the scanned public text, so none was added |
| Repository microsite | Old math-engine and license-first positioning | company, product, proof, and evaluation language aligned to luxiedge.com |
| Provider details | Historical evidence pages exposed public provider endpoints | connection endpoints and device identifiers omitted while preserving device and result scope |

# Erock Suite Overview

**Mission:** Turn code + flexible load into dispatchable, verifiable capacity.

## Products
- **Erock Edge™** — appliance-class controller for small sites (retrofit-friendly).
- **Erock SDG™** — Software-Defined Generator control for SMB to enterprise (market-agnostic core).
- **Erock Core™** — portfolio & data-center orchestration; ISO/RTO-facing adapters and analytics.

## Economics (illustrative)
- **SMB (25 kW peak)**: ~$2.8k/year benefit; ~$7k install; ~2.5-year simple payback.
- **Industrial/DC (50 MW)**: ~$1.2M/year total benefit potential; <2-year simple payback (site dependent).

## Why now
Capacity constraints, compute growth, DR/VPP policy momentum, and falling controls cost.
